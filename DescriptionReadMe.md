# SIREN

**An AI-powered device that helps prevent child kidnapping — before it's too late.**

---

## The Problem

Since 2024, cases of missing and kidnapped children have been rising sharply in Cameroon. Search alerts for missing children circulate constantly on WhatsApp and Facebook — a photo, a name, a last known location, a parent begging for help. These are not rare, isolated stories anymore. They are a near-daily occurrence, and they are almost always shared *after* the child has already disappeared.

Existing tracking solutions only show a dot on a map. They don't understand what's actually happening to the child, and they stop working the moment mobile network coverage drops — which is exactly when and where these situations tend to occur.

## Our Solution

SIREN is a small, discreet wearable device, paired with a mobile app, built around one core idea: **detect danger before it becomes a disappearance, not after.**

The device continuously tracks the child's location and movement. An AI engine, running on our server, learns each child's individual daily habits — their routine trips, the places they usually go, their normal schedule — and uses this understanding to spot behavior that doesn't fit: an unexpected detour, an unusual speed, leaving a familiar area, or losing signal in a suspicious way. When something looks wrong, parents are alerted immediately, with a clear explanation of what triggered the alert.

Crucially, SIREN doesn't depend on a single point of failure. If mobile network is unavailable, the device automatically switches to a Bluetooth relay, using nearby SIREN devices to keep transmitting the child's position — so protection doesn't disappear just because the network does.

## Why It's Different

Most child-safety trackers on the market — in Cameroon, across Africa, and globally — are passive. They show a location and little else. SIREN is the first system in this space designed to be both **intelligent** (learning and adapting to each child individually) and **resilient** (still working when connectivity fails), which matters enormously in regions where stable network coverage cannot be taken for granted.

## The Team

SIREN is built by a small, technically-driven team combining skills in artificial intelligence and embedded electronics — the two disciplines needed to bring this device to life, from the hardware itself to the AI that powers it.

## Our Motivation

This project didn't start as a business idea. It started from something much simpler: scrolling through WhatsApp and Facebook and seeing, again and again, families desperately searching for a missing child. At some point, a question became impossible to ignore — why do we only ever react after a child is already gone, when the technology to detect danger *before* that point already exists? SIREN is our answer to that question.

## Impact

Child kidnapping and disappearance sit within a much larger global challenge: rising insecurity and violence that leave families living in fear, with children among the most vulnerable. SIREN contributes directly to **UN Sustainable Development Goal 16 — Peace, Justice, and Strong Institutions** — by giving families a proactive tool to prevent harm, rather than only a way to search after the harm is already done.
